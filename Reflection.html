Reflection

Lagos challenges stereotypes about African cities. It is not simply chaotic or poor but a complex megacity with strong economic sectors and vibrant culture.

Tensions in the City

Some residents benefit from economic growth, while others face insecurity and high living costs.

Avoiding Simplifications

European visitors should avoid seeing Nigeria as a homogeneous country. Lagos alone contains many different realities.

Sources

World Bank articles, Lagos State Government information, academic research on informal settlements, aerial photographs, market images, Makoko photos.
