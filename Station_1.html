Lagos: A City Shaped by Migration

Lagos grew from a small coastal settlement into a megacity due to massive migration from all parts of Nigeria. Today, more than 20 million people live in the city, representing hundreds of ethnic groups, languages and cultural backgrounds. Migration continues to shape the city’s identity, economy and social landscape.

Diversity as Identity

Lagos is a symbol of modern Nigerian identity. The city’s youth culture, fashion, music, digital media and entrepreneurial spirit influence trends across the entire country. Lagos is constantly changing — shaped by new arrivals, new ideas and new opportunities.

Key Facts & Statistics

• Over 20 million inhabitants

• One of the fastest-growing cities in Africa

• Hundreds of ethnic groups represented

• Major migration destination within Nigeria
