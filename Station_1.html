Lagos: A City Shaped by Migration

Lagos grew from a small coastal settlement into a megacity due to massive migration from all parts of Nigeria. This created a highly diverse population with many ethnic groups, languages and religions.

Diversity as Identity

Lagos is a symbol of modern Nigerian identity, shaped by youth culture, fashion, media and constant change.
