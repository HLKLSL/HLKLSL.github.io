Life on the Lagoon

Makoko is often called a “floating slum”. Many houses stand on stilts, and residents use wooden boats for transport.

Challenges & Resilience

Despite poverty and lack of services, Makoko has strong social networks, schools and religious institutions.

Makoko as a Symbol

The community illustrates broader issues in Nigeria: inequality, land rights conflicts and vulnerability to eviction.
