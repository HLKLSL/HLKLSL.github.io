Exhibition Introduction

Lagos is Nigeria’s fastest‑moving, most diverse, and most influential city. As a megacity of more than twenty million people, it shapes the country’s economy, culture, and identity like no other place. Understanding Lagos means understanding the forces that drive modern Nigeria.

This exhibition focuses on how everyday life in Lagos is formed by diversity, ambition, and rapid urban change. By exploring its streets, waterways, and creative scenes, visitors gain insight into the energy and challenges that define Nigeria today.
